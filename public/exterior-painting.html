Service · Exterior

A paint job built for west-side weather

Rain, sun and freeze-thaw attack the outside from every direction at once. Exterior paint earns its years in the prep — cleaning, scraping, caulking — before the finish coats.

What gets covered

The usual scope

Siding

Washed, scraped, spot-primed and coated — lap siding and flat surfaces, worked so the lap lines stay clean.

Trim, fascia & soffits

The edges that decide how the house reads — caulked tight and painted smooth.

Doors & porch surfaces

Entry doors, porch rails and columns get the same finish system as the rest of the shell.

Gutter & downspout color

Coordinated or contrast — decided in the scope so the hardware matches the plan.

Good questions

Before you call

What surfaces does an exterior job cover?

Siding, trim, fascia, soffits, doors and porch surfaces — the shell of the house, painted as one system, not one wall at a time.

Is the outside cleaned before painting?

Exterior prep starts with cleaning, then scraping and caulking, before any new coat goes on. Paint over dirt or open gaps peels right back off.

When is the best time to paint the outside?

West-side scheduling follows the dry stretches. Call (360) 798-2333 when the project is on your calendar and we'll talk timing against the season.

Can this be combined with an interior job?

Interior and exterior are commonly scoped together — one crew, one schedule, one walkthrough at the end.
