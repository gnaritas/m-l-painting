Service · Interior

Interior paint that survives real life

Hall scuffs, kitchen grease, closet-door edges — interior paint fails at the prep, not the color. The prep gets fixed first, then the coat goes on.

What gets covered

The usual scope

Walls & ceilings

Full rooms or accent walls — roller coverage on walls, brush work where the roller can't reach.

Trim, base & doors

Cut lines at ceiling and base, gloss on trim and doors so every edge reads sharp.

Hallways & stairs

Worn high-touch walls and banisters, worked section by section so the house keeps functioning.

Closets & bonus rooms

The cheap fast rooms too — primed and coated, not left as an afterthought.

Good questions

Before you call

What surfaces does an interior job cover?

Room walls and ceilings, hallways, closets, trim, baseboards and doors — the full envelope of the rooms in scope, not just the big flat walls.

Is patching and prep included?

It comes first: nail pops and patches filled, sanding smooth, dust killed, priming where bare or repaired. That is the half of the job cheap quotes skip.

Do you move furniture out of the way?

Furniture gets shifted, covered and worked around as part of prep. Empty rooms go faster, but it is not required.
